# Demand Curve Growth & Conversion Analysis

## Goal
Create an unlisted standalone page at `/demand-curve-analysis` that presents Steve’s independent assessment of Demand Curve’s landing-page offer. The page will borrow the reference page’s strong editorial pacing and conversion-focused structure without copying its branding or layout verbatim.

## Page experience
- Open with a compact branded masthead, a clear “Independent analysis” label, and the title “Demand Curve — Growth & Conversion Analysis.”
- Frame the assessment around Steve’s operating loop: Observe → Identify friction → Prioritize leverage → Form a hypothesis → Test → Measure downstream impact.
- Present the seven recommendations in a highly scannable editorial format:
  - Immediate opportunities: buyer qualification and earlier proof.
  - Longer-term opportunities: segmented acquisition experiences and a productized Signal diagnostic.
  - Strategic theses: conversion systems, compounding intelligence, and conversion economics.
- Give each tactical recommendation a consistent structure: Observation, Hypothesis, Experiment, Primary measures, and Why it matters.
- Use restrained interactive details such as a sticky section index, active reading progress, and compact metric/proof treatments. Motion will respect reduced-motion settings.
- End with the prioritized Now → Next → Then sequence, the larger operating takeaway, and a direct contact action.

## Visual direction
- Keep the site’s dark executive palette, typography, semantic tokens, and high-contrast editorial character.
- Adapt the reference’s strongest patterns: concise first screen, numbered modules, early proof, clear service-system diagrams, generous spacing, and repeated decision points.
- Avoid reproducing Demand Curve branding, proprietary artwork, or exact copy outside the user-provided analysis.
- Label the page as Steve Peele II’s independent analysis so authorship and affiliation are clear.

## Visibility and routing
- Add one TanStack route at `/demand-curve-analysis` with complete route-specific metadata.
- Mark it `noindex, nofollow` because it is intended to be hosted but unlisted.
- Do not add it to the header, footer, homepage, sitemap-like navigation, or entrance flow.
- Internal actions will point only to existing Contact and Experience pages; no new backend or form work is needed.

- Confirm the page is absent from shared navigation and footer.
