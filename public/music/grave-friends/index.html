The projectFriends in a dark room

The ache, carried together. That is the whole arrangement.

Grave Friends is an Ohio melodic hardcore project. Not metalcore aggression, not hardcore posturing. The songs lean melodic and honest, written the way people actually talk to each other when something hurts.

Steve Peele II runs the project and its Instagram. The recorded catalog is real and out in the world: six releases on Apple Music, with the Spotify artist page matched through Compathy. There is no Bandcamp page for this project. Four of the releases are named in the list below.

Underground and communal is not a costume for this one. It is the format: a dark room, a few voices, one beam of light, and nobody carrying the heavy part by themselves.
