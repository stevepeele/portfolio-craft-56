A Book of Record — No Community, No Network

The Lab

The internet's builder scene is an echo chamber — engagement farmers, pseudo-friendships, everybody linking to everybody else's thing. This is the ledger underneath it. Who people are. What they're building. Whether they're still showing up. Nothing else.

§ 01 — Protocol

The Rules

What this is

  • A record. Kept, not performed.
  • Continuity over time — progress you can trace, entry by entry.
  • A reference surface. Link to it from anywhere; it points back to nothing.
  • Building in public without the performance.

What this is not

  • No DMs. No comments. No replies.
  • No feed. No algorithm. No engagement metrics.
  • No followers, no counts, no leaderboards.
  • No networking. No cross-promo machinery.
